Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SDL loans.

What is SDL (Shubh Dhan Lakshmi)?
+

SDL is a friendly loan platform for India that provides short-term salary-backed loans. You borrow now and repay on your next salary date via UPI — no long forms, no bank visits.

How much can I borrow?
+

Your loan limit depends on your salary profile and repayment history. New users start with a modest limit that grows with each on-time repayment. Admins review and approve each loan within 24 hours.

What charges apply?
+

SDL charges are fully transparent: 2% interest, 8% portal charge, and 9% processing fee. The total repayment amount is shown before you request the loan. On timely repayment, you get 1% back in your wallet.

How do I receive the loan?
+

Once approved, the loan amount is transferred directly to your UPI ID within 24 hours. You'll receive a WhatsApp notification and email confirmation with your agreement.

What happens if I'm late?
+

A daily overdue charge applies for late payments. We'll send reminders via WhatsApp before your due date. Consistent on-time repayments increase your loan limit for future borrowing.

How does the referral program work?
+

Share your referral link with friends. When they register and repay their first loan, you earn 1% of their loan amount — credited automatically to your wallet.

Is my data safe?
+

Yes. All personal and financial data is encrypted and stored securely. We use your information only for loan processing and KYC verification. We never sell your data to third parties.
